# Sample Query → Response Examples
Below are examples of the kind of queries BookLeaf receives and how responses should be framed. Use
these to calibrate your AI’s output.

| Category | Sample Author Query | Expected Response Tone & Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Royalty & Payments** | I published my book 4 months ago and still haven’t received any royalty. What’s going on? | Acknowledge the frustration. Explain the quarterly royalty cycle and 45-day payout window. Check if their bank details are linked. Provide the specific next payout date. If the payment is genuinely overdue, escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline. |
| **Royalty & Payments** | My royalty amount seems too low. I sold 200 copies but only received ₹3,000. | Explain the net profit calculation: MRP minus printing, platform commission, and shipping. Offer to share a detailed line-by-line royalty breakdown. Don’t be defensive—offer transparency. |
| **ISBN & Metadata** | My book is showing a different ISBN on Amazon than what’s on the physical copy. | Treat as high priority. Acknowledge this is a serious data issue. Confirm you are escalating to the production team immediately. Provide a 48-hour resolution timeline. |
| **Printing & Quality** | I received my author copies and the print quality is terrible. The images are blurry and pages are misaligned. | Apologise sincerely. Ask the author to share photos of the defective copies. Confirm that BookLeaf will arrange a free reprint once verified. Provide a timeline of 5–7 business days for the reprint. |
| **Distribution** | My book is published but it’s showing as “Currently Unavailable” on Amazon. | Explain that this is typically a stock sync issue. Confirm you’re triggering a re-sync with the distribution team. Set expectation: 24–48 hours for it to go live again. |
| **Production Updates** | It’s been 3 weeks and my book is still in typesetting. When will it be done? | Check the actual production status. If it’s delayed, be honest about why (e.g., waiting on author approval for proofs). Provide a specific updated timeline. Don’t blame the author even if the delay is on their side—frame it collaboratively. |
| **General** | Can I update the description of my book on Amazon after it’s already live? | Yes, metadata updates can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team. Changes typically reflect on platforms within 3–5 business days. |
